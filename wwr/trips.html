Featured Trips

Wild Canyon Expedition

Duration: 3 Days / 2 Nights

Difficulty: Advanced (Class IV-V)

Experience the thrill of Colorado River's most intense rapids. This multi-day adventure includes camping under the stars, gourmet riverside meals, and expert guides. Perfect for adrenaline seekers looking for a true wilderness experience.

Includes: All gear, meals, camping equipment, and professional guide.

Family Float & Fun

Duration: Half Day (4 hours)

Difficulty: Beginner (Class I-II)

Perfect for families and first-timers! Enjoy gentle rapids, swimming opportunities, and stunning mountain views. Our guides share stories about local wildlife and geology. Safe fun for all ages (minimum age 6).

Includes: Life jackets, splash gear, and snack break.

Extreme Rapids Challenge

Duration: Full Day (8 hours)

Difficulty: Expert (Class V)

Push your limits on the legendary Gauley River. Over 100 rapids including Class V monsters. Designed for experienced paddlers in top physical condition. Small groups ensure maximum safety and adrenaline.

Includes: Professional guide, action photography, and riverside lunch.
