History

Exciting Expeditions was founded in 1995 by a group of adventure enthusiasts who wanted to share their love for the great outdoors. Starting with just a handful of rafts and a passion for exploration, this company has grown into a leading provider of whitewater rafting experiences. Over the decades, we've navigated the wildest rivers, trained world-class guides, and built a community of thrill-seekers who cherish both adrenaline and environmental stewardship.

From our first descent down the Colorado River to expanding operations across six states, our commitment to safety and authentic adventure remains unchanged. We honor the legacy of exploration while embracing innovation — offering eco-friendly rafting practices, custom expeditions, and family-friendly trips. The roaring rapids we conquered then still fuel our passion today, ensuring every guest experiences the raw beauty of untamed rivers.
